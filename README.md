# Hospital Emergency Room Monthly Analytics Dashboard 

##  Executive Summary
This **Healthcare Analytics Dashboard** provides a detailed operational overview of hospital emergency room performance. It helps hospital administrators track patient throughput, monitor wait times, evaluate department referral distribution, and assess overall patient satisfaction to optimize emergency care delivery[cite: 12].

---

##  Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
* **Total Number of Patients:** 513[cite: 12]
* **Average Wait Time:** 36.32 minutes[cite: 12]
* **Patient Satisfaction Score:** 4.96 / 10[cite: 12]
* **Admission Rate:** 52% Admitted (269 Patients) vs 48% Not Admitted (244 Patients)[cite: 12]
* **Attendance Efficiency:** 62% Delayed vs 38% On Time[cite: 12]
* **Gender Ratio:** 53% Male vs 47% Female[cite: 12]

---

##  Tools & Technologies Used
* **Data Visualization Tool:** Microsoft Excel / Power BI Desktop[cite: 12].
* **Interactive Slicers:** Year slicers (2023 / 2024) and Monthly slicers (Jan to Dec) for dynamic time-series filtering[cite: 12].
* **Visual Elements:** Sparkline charts, KPI cards, Donut distribution charts, Bar/Column charts, and Status progress bars[cite: 12].

---

##  Dashboard Visualizations & Key Features

1. **Patient Volume & Operational KPIs:**
   * **Total Patients (513)**, **Average Wait Time (36.32 mins)**, and **Satisfaction Score (4.96)** with embedded trend sparklines[cite: 12].
2. **Admission Status Breakdown:**
   * Real-time tracking of patient disposition: **Admitted (269 patients / 52.44%)** vs **Not Admitted (244 patients / 47.56%)**[cite: 12].
3. **Attendance Timeliness & Gender Analysis:**
   * **Patient Attended with Time:** Identifies operational delays (**62% Delayed** vs **38% On Time**)[cite: 12].
   * **Gender Distribution:** Demographic breakdown showing **53% Male** and **47% Female** emergency patients[cite: 12].
4. **Demographic Distribution by Age Group:**
   * Categorizes patient counts across age brackets: **0–09 (76)**, **10–19 (69)**, **50–59 (66)**, **60–69 (67)**, **20–29 (64)**, **30–39 (59)**, **40–49 (58)**, and **70–79 (54)**[cite: 12].
5. **Department Referral Distribution:**
   * Tracks patient referrals: **None (299)**, **General Practice (103)**, **Orthopedics (65)**, **Cardiology (14)**, **Physiotherapy (14)**, **Neurology (9)**, **Renal (5)**, and **Gastroenterology (4)**[cite: 12].

##  Key Operational Insights
* **High Delay Rate:** 62% of patients experience delays in being attended to, indicating potential bottlenecks in emergency triage or initial consultation[cite: 12].
* **Pediatric Care Demand:** The highest concentration of emergency visits occurs in the **0–09 age group (76 patients)**, followed by senior age brackets (50–69 years)[cite: 12].
* **Referral Breakdown:** Over 58% of patients required no further department referral (**299 patients**), while **General Practice** and **Orthopedics** represent the majority of specialized referrals[cite: 12].
